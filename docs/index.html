Made for every kind of Scouting recognition

Leaders use this certificate for Eagle Scout courts of honor, rank advancement, Arrow of Light and Cub Scout awards, merit badge and service project milestones, summer camp honors, and thanking Scoutmasters, den leaders, committee members, and parent volunteers. It works for Scouts BSA troops, Cub Scout packs, Venturing crews, Sea Scout ships, districts, and councils.

How to use it

Type in the fields on the left and the preview updates as you go. When it looks right, press Download PDF and print it at home or take the file to a print shop. Heavy cream or parchment card stock (65 to 110 lb) shows off the artwork best. Leave the signature lines blank in the form if your leaders will sign by hand; the lines still print as long as a name or title is entered.

About the artwork

The border was drawn by hand by Harold Sachs, a Scouting volunteer, and is shared free for recognizing Scouts, leaders, and volunteers. Please do not sell the artwork or certificates made from it.

Your privacy

The PDF is built inside your web browser. Names and citations are never uploaded or stored on a server. Your last entries are kept only in this browser so a refresh does not erase your work; Clear removes them.
