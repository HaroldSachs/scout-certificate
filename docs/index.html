Good for just about any Scouting award

Use it for an Eagle Scout court of honor, a rank advancement, the Arrow of Light, merit badges, a service project, or a great week at summer camp. It’s also a nice way to thank the Scoutmaster, den leader, committee member or parent who always shows up. Troops, packs, Venturing crews and Sea Scout ships are all welcome to use it.

Printing tips

Fill in the form and watch the certificate change as you type. When you like it, hit Download PDF. It prints fine at home, but heavy cream or parchment card stock (65 to 110 lb) really makes the colors pop. Want your leaders to sign it by hand? Put their names and titles in the signature boxes, and they can sign right above the line at the ceremony.

About the artwork

I’m Harold Sachs, a digital artist. This border is my update of a classic 1950s Scout certificate, and I’m happy to share it with any unit, free. All I ask is that you don’t sell the artwork or certificates made from it.

Your information stays with you

Your certificate is made right in your web browser, so the names you type never leave your computer. The page remembers what you typed last, so you can come back and finish later, and the Clear button wipes it. I keep a simple count of visits and downloads, with no cookies and nothing personal, just so I know people are enjoying it.
